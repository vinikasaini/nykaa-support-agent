Document: Exchange Policy

Customers can request an exchange for eligible products within the applicable
exchange period after delivery.

The product must generally be unused, unopened, and in its original packaging
with all applicable tags and accessories intact.

Exchange availability depends on the product category, item eligibility, and
the availability of a suitable replacement.

If the requested replacement is unavailable, the customer may be offered an
alternative resolution according to the applicable policy.

Products that have been used, damaged after delivery, or do not meet the
applicable exchange conditions may not qualify for an exchange.

For damaged, defective, or incorrect products, customers should report the
issue as soon as possible and provide the information requested by support.

Exchange requests are subject to verification and applicable eligibility
conditions.
