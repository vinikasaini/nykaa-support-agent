Document: Delivery Policy

Delivery timelines depend on the customer's location, product availability,
seller processing time, and the selected delivery service.

The estimated delivery date shown at the time of ordering is an estimate and
may change because of operational or external factors.

Customers can track an order using the order tracking information available
in their account.

If an order has been shipped, the customer can use the available tracking
details to monitor its progress.

If the estimated delivery date has passed and the order has not been
delivered, the customer should check the latest tracking information before
contacting support.

Delivery may take longer during high-demand periods, sales events, holidays,
or unexpected logistical disruptions.

Customers should ensure that the delivery address and contact information
provided with the order are accurate.
