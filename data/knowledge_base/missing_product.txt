Document: Missing or Incomplete Order Policy

If a customer receives an order with a missing item, they should report the
issue to customer support as soon as possible.

The customer should provide the relevant order details and any information
requested by support to help investigate the issue.

Support may review the order information, shipment details, and other
available records before determining the appropriate resolution.

If an item was not included in the shipment, the resolution may include
delivery of the missing item, a refund, or another applicable resolution.

Customers should retain the delivered products and packaging until the issue
has been reviewed, as these may be required during the investigation.

If the package appears damaged or tampered with when delivered, the customer
should report this information along with the missing-item complaint.
