Document: Cash on Delivery Policy

Cash on Delivery (COD) may be available only for eligible products,
locations, and orders.

The availability of COD is determined during checkout based on the delivery
address, product eligibility, and other applicable conditions.

Customers should have the required payment amount available when the order is
delivered.

If a COD order is returned and the return is approved, the refund process may
require the customer to provide valid bank account or other payment details.

COD availability can change depending on the customer's location, order
value, product type, or other operational conditions.

If COD is not available for an order, the customer can select another payment
method shown during checkout.
