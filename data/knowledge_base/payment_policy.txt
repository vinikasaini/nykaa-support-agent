Nykaa Support Knowledge Base
Document: Payment Policy

Customers can use the payment methods displayed as available during the
checkout process.

A payment may fail because of incorrect payment information, insufficient
funds, bank restrictions, technical issues, or temporary payment gateway
problems.

If a payment fails, customers should first verify their payment details and
check whether the amount has been deducted from their account.

If money was deducted but the order was not successfully placed, the payment
may be reversed or refunded according to the applicable payment process.

Customers should avoid making repeated payments if they are unsure whether
the previous transaction was successful.

For payment-related issues, customers should keep the transaction reference,
order details, and relevant payment information available when contacting
support.

The final payment status should be checked through the customer's order
details or the applicable payment provider.
