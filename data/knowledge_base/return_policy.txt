Document: Return Policy

Customers can request a return for eligible products within 7 days of delivery.

The product must be unused, unopened, and in its original packaging with all
original tags and accessories intact.

Certain products may not be eligible for return due to hygiene or safety
considerations. These may include opened cosmetics, personal care products,
and other products where resale is not appropriate.

To request a return, the customer should use the order details available in
their account and select the applicable return option.

After a return request is submitted, the product may be inspected before the
return is approved.

If the return is approved, a reverse pickup may be arranged where available.
The refund process starts after the returned product passes the required
inspection.

For damaged, defective, or incorrect products, customers should report the
issue as soon as possible and provide the requested details or photographs.
