Document: Refund Policy

Refunds are initiated after an approved return has been received and the
returned product has successfully completed the required inspection.

The time taken for the refund to reach the customer depends on the original
payment method and the customer's bank or payment provider.

For prepaid orders, the refund is generally processed back to the original
payment method.

For Cash on Delivery orders, the customer may need to provide valid bank
account or other required payment details to receive the refund.

If a refund has been initiated but the customer has not received it within
the expected processing period, the customer should contact support with the
relevant order details.

Refunds are not guaranteed for products that fail the applicable return
eligibility or inspection requirements.
