Document: Loyalty and Rewards Policy

Eligible customers may receive loyalty benefits, rewards, or promotional
offers based on the applicable program terms.

Rewards and promotional benefits may have specific eligibility conditions,
validity periods, minimum purchase requirements, or product exclusions.

Customers should check the applicable offer or rewards information associated
with their account to understand the conditions of a particular benefit.

Promotional offers cannot always be combined with other discounts or
promotional codes.

If a reward or promotional benefit is not applied as expected, the customer
should provide the relevant order or offer details when contacting support.

Rewards, offers, and their terms may change over time and are subject to the
conditions applicable when the offer is used.
