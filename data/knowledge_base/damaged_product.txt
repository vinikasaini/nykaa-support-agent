Document: Damaged or Defective Product Policy

Customers should report a product that arrives damaged, defective, leaking, or
otherwise unusable as soon as possible after delivery.

The customer may be asked to provide photographs or other information showing
the condition of the product and its packaging.

Support may review the information provided before deciding the appropriate
resolution.

Depending on the circumstances and product eligibility, the resolution may
include a replacement, exchange, return, refund, or another applicable
resolution.

Customers should retain the original product, packaging, tags, and
accessories until the issue has been reviewed and resolved.

Damage caused after delivery through misuse, improper storage, or normal wear
may not qualify for a replacement or refund.

If the issue involves an incorrect product being delivered, the customer
should report the issue promptly with the relevant order details.
