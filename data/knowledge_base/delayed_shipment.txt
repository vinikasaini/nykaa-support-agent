Document: Delayed Shipment Policy

An order may be delayed because of high order volumes, seller processing
delays, transportation issues, weather conditions, or other logistical
disruptions.

Customers should first check the latest order tracking information to
determine whether the shipment is still moving through the delivery network.

If the estimated delivery date has passed and there has been no meaningful
tracking update, the customer should contact support with the order details.

A delayed shipment does not automatically mean that the order has been
cancelled.

If an order cannot be delivered because of a logistics issue, the customer
may receive further instructions or an applicable resolution from support.

Customers should keep their registered phone number available because the
delivery partner may need to contact them during the delivery process.
