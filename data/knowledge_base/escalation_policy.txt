Nykaa Support Knowledge Base
Document: Customer Support Escalation Policy

Customers should contact support when an issue cannot be resolved through the
standard self-service options.

Support may require the customer's order ID, registered contact information,
transaction details, or other relevant information to investigate the issue.

Issues involving damaged products, missing items, incorrect orders, failed
payments, delayed refunds, or unresolved delivery problems may require
additional investigation.

If an issue cannot be resolved at the first level of support, it may be
escalated to the appropriate support team for further review.

Customers should provide accurate information and avoid submitting multiple
requests for the same issue unless instructed by support.

The final resolution depends on the order details, applicable policies, and
the outcome of the investigation.
