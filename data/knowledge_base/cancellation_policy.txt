Document: Cancellation Policy

Customers can request cancellation of an order before the order has been
dispatched.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible
through the normal cancellation process.

If cancellation is successfully completed before dispatch, the customer will
not receive the cancelled items.

For prepaid orders, an eligible refund for a successfully cancelled order is
processed to the original payment method.

If the order has already been shipped, the customer may need to receive the
order and follow the applicable return process instead.

Cancellation availability can depend on the current status of the order.
Customers should check their order details for the available cancellation
option.
